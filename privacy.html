Alfolio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your library stays on your device

Alfolio stores bookmarks, collections, tags, feed entries, settings, and cached images on your Mac. The app does not require an account and does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, or a cloud synchronization service. It does not upload your library or document contents to the developer.

Connections to websites

To retrieve titles, descriptions, cover images, feeds, and article content, Alfolio connects to the corresponding websites and image servers. These requests can occur in the background when you import bookmarks, browse your library, or refresh feeds. Servers receive the requested URL, your IP address, and ordinary connection metadata. Loading external images can reveal that you viewed an item.

Opening a website in the preview loads that website and its resources. Websites may store cookies or other local data and process information under their own policies. Limited ad and tracker filtering is provided; it does not block all third-party requests or guarantee that a site does not track visitors.

Google Translate

When you choose the Google Translate action, the original page URL is sent to Google and the translated page is loaded through Google's service. Do not use this action for private or confidential URLs you do not want to share with Google. Google's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Files and clipboard

The Mac App Store edition accesses document folders you explicitly select. Alfolio can retain a macOS security-scoped bookmark locally to reopen a selected folder after restart. Import, export, and backup operations access files or folders you choose. The clipboard is read when you invoke an operation that needs its text. Document browsing does not upload local file contents.

Backups and deletion

Backups and exported HTML files contain library information and are not encrypted by Alfolio. Protect these files when copying or sharing them. If you select a cloud-synchronized backup location, its provider may receive the backup according to that provider's settings.

You can remove individual bookmarks and reset the library in Settings. Resetting the library does not necessarily remove website cookies, every cached image, or copies of exported backups. Backups must be deleted from their saved locations. Website data is stored separately in the app's local profile; removing the app alone may leave that profile behind.
