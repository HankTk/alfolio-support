About this policy

Alfolio is a Mac application by Folio Grove for organizing bookmarks and reading feeds. This policy explains data handled by the app, the websites it connects to, this support website, and support email. Optional local-document features, where available, are also covered.

Alfolio does not require a Folio Grove account. The app has no developer-operated advertising, analytics, or cloud library synchronization, and it does not send your library or local documents to Folio Grove.

These categories are stored locally by the app or its embedded browser. A local profile is not an encrypted vault. Your Mac account, device security and any device or cloud backup services you use also affect access to this data.

Website, image and feed requests

Alfolio contacts the websites and image servers associated with your links to retrieve page titles, descriptions, photos, feed entries and article content. It may also check whether saved links are reachable. Requests can happen in the background during library checks, imports, browsing and feed refreshes. Viewing an item can trigger a request for its image or page information.

Destination servers receive your IP address, requested URL and ordinary connection information such as a user-agent. URLs can contain private identifiers or access tokens. Websites may load resources from additional providers and process information under their own policies. Alfolio does not guarantee that every request is anonymous or that all tracking is blocked.

Site icons may be requested automatically from Google’s favicon service. Those requests include the site hostname and ordinary connection information, including your IP address; Google’s privacy policy applies. This icon lookup is separate from the optional translation action.

Photos and article content remain subject to their owners’ rights and the source website’s terms. Saving a link or displaying a thumbnail does not grant permission to republish its content.

Website sign-in and cookies

Pages opened in the preview and website popup windows use the app’s embedded browser session. They can share cookies and other website storage, and a website may keep you signed in after you close a window or restart Alfolio.

Credentials entered into a website are handled by that website. Alfolio does not offer a password-manager feature or send website passwords to Folio Grove. The app’s browser session is separate from Safari or Chrome, so signing in or out there does not necessarily change your Alfolio session.

Use the website’s own sign-out control when finished. Closing a popup does not by itself delete cookies or end a website session. Authentication compatibility depends on the website; you can use your normal browser if a service does not work in the preview.

Optional Google Translate

Translation runs only when you choose the Google Translate action. The original page URL is sent to Google, and translated content is loaded through Google’s service. Do not translate private, token-bearing or confidential URLs that you do not want to share with Google. Google’s privacy policy and service terms apply.

Files, clipboard and permissions

Import, export and backup actions access files or folders you select. Where the Documents feature is available, the Mac App Store edition accesses a document folder you explicitly choose and can retain a local macOS security-scoped bookmark to reopen that folder. It does not upload local document contents to Folio Grove.

Alfolio reads clipboard text when you invoke an action that uses it, such as adding a URL from the clipboard. It does not provide continuous clipboard monitoring.

Retention, backups and deletion

Your library remains on your Mac until you remove data, reset the relevant library records or remove the app’s stored profile. Cached images and website data can remain separately. Alfolio does not operate a remote copy of your library that support can restore.

  • Delete individual bookmarks or remove feed subscriptions using the app’s controls.
  • The current Back Up Library feature exports bookmarks, collections and tags. It does not back up feeds, website cookies or signed-in sessions. Exported files and backup folders are not encrypted by Alfolio.
  • The current Reset All Data action clears bookmark, collection and tag records and app preferences. It is not a complete device-data wipe: feeds, browser cookies, cached files and exported backups may remain.
  • Sign out of websites separately. There is currently no in-app control that clears all browser cookies and website storage.
  • Removing the app alone may leave its profile behind. For complete profile removal, contact support for instructions appropriate to your installed edition. Back up data first; profile removal can be irreversible.

Copies you save to iCloud Drive, another cloud folder, Time Machine or another backup system are handled by that provider or system. Remove those copies separately when appropriate.

Support email and this website

If you email support, Folio Grove receives your email address, message and attachments. We use them to investigate your request and follow up. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, account balances, complete private libraries, provisioning profiles or signing keys. Redact private information in screenshots and logs.

The support mailbox uses Gmail, so correspondence is also processed by Google under its policies. We retain support correspondence while needed for the request and follow-up, or where required by law. You may request correction or deletion of information you sent us; applicable legal obligations may limit deletion. We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your Mac.

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no Folio Grove analytics, advertising, contact form or third-party fonts. GitHub may process technical visitor information when serving the site; see the GitHub privacy statement.
